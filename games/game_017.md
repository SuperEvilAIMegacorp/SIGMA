# Hex Conquest

## Game Information
- **Genre**: Abstract Strategy
- **Players**: 2
- **Duration**: 20-30 minutes
- **Complexity**: 5/10 (Medium)

## Objective
Create a connected path of your pieces from your side to the opposite side.

## Components
- Hexagonal board (11x11 hexes)
- 60 black pieces
- 60 white pieces

## Setup
1. Empty board placed between players
2. Each player owns two opposite sides (marked)
3. Black goes first

## Rules
1. On your turn, place one piece on any empty hex
2. Pieces cannot be moved once placed
3. You win by connecting your two sides with a continuous chain of your pieces
4. Chains connect through edge-adjacent hexes (not corners)

## Winning Condition
First player to form a connected path between their two sides wins.

## Special Rules
- The pie rule: After black's first move, white may choose to swap colors instead of making their first move
- Ties are impossible due to board topology
