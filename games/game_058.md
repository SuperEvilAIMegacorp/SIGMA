# Labyrinth Shift

## Game Information
- **Genre**: Puzzle Strategy
- **Players**: 2-4
- **Duration**: 30-40 minutes
- **Complexity**: 6/10 (Medium-Complex)

## Objective
Navigate the shifting maze to collect treasures and reach the exit.

## Components
- 49 maze tiles (7x7 grid, walls on various sides)
- 1 extra tile (used to shift maze)
- Player pawns
- 20 treasure tokens
- 20 treasure cards (showing which treasures to collect)

## Setup
1. Randomly arrange 49 tiles to form maze
2. Place treasures on designated tiles
3. Each player draws 4 treasure cards (secret objectives)
4. Place pawns at corners

## Rules
1. On your turn:
   - Insert the extra tile into any row or column (pushes tiles)
   - This shifts the entire maze row/column
   - Tile that gets pushed out becomes new extra tile
   - Move your pawn through open passages (as far as walls allow)
2. If you reach a treasure on your card, collect it and draw new card
3. Can't immediately undo previous player's shift

## Winning Condition
First player to collect all treasures on their cards and reach exit wins.

## Special Rules
- Walls block movement
- Can meet on same tile (no blocking)
- Some tiles have special properties:
  - Teleports: jump to another teleport
  - One-way passages
  - Rotating tiles (pivot instead of shifting)
- Advanced: add secret passages and locked doors
