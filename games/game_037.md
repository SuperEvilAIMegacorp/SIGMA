# Connection Web

## Game Information
- **Genre**: Abstract Strategy
- **Players**: 2-3
- **Duration**: 15-20 minutes
- **Complexity**: 5/10 (Medium)

## Objective
Create the largest connected network of your pieces.

## Components
- 7x7 grid board
- 21 pieces per player (in 3 colors)
- Connection scoring reference

## Setup
1. Empty board placed in center
2. Each player takes pieces in their color
3. Randomly determine player order

## Rules
1. On your turn, place 1-2 pieces on empty spaces
2. Pieces connect if they're orthogonally adjacent (not diagonal)
3. Score your largest connected group at end of game
4. Take turns until all pieces are placed

## Winning Condition
Largest connected group wins. Ties broken by second-largest group.

## Special Rules
- Playing 2 pieces costs your next turn (skip once)
- Cannot place piece that would connect opponent's groups
- Corner spaces worth bonus +1 to group size if controlled
- Center space worth +2 bonus

## Scoring Example
If your largest connected group is 8 pieces and includes 1 corner + center:
Score = 8 + 1 + 2 = 11 points
