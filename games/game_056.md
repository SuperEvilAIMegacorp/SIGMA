# Rumor Mill

## Game Information
- **Genre**: Social Deduction
- **Players**: 4-8
- **Duration**: 25-35 minutes
- **Complexity**: 5/10 (Medium)

## Objective
Spread your rumor to the most players while identifying the source of rumors targeting you.

## Components
- Role cards (1 per player, identifies your target)
- 80 rumor cards (various statements)
- Identity tokens
- Truth tracker
- Accusation tokens (3 per player)

## Setup
1. Each player draws a role card (shows who they're targeting with rumors)
2. Shuffle rumor deck
3. Each player draws 5 rumor cards

## Rules
1. **Whisper Phase (3 minutes):**
   - Move around, whisper rumors to other players
   - Can share any rumor cards you hold
   - Can embellish or add rumors
2. **Accusation Phase:**
   - Each player can spend tokens to accuse who targeted them
   - Correct accusation: +3 points, target loses 2 points
   - Wrong accusation: -1 point
3. **Scoring Phase:**
   - Score 1 point per player who heard your rumor
4. Play 4 rounds (new target each round)

## Winning Condition
Most points after 4 rounds wins.

## Special Rules
- Must spread at least 2 rumors per round
- Can spread rumors that aren't yours (misinformation)
- Some rumors are marked "true" - can't contradict these
- Counter-rumor mechanic: convince others the rumor is false
