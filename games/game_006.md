# Memory Chain

## Game Information
- **Genre**: Memory Game
- **Players**: 2-6
- **Duration**: 10-15 minutes
- **Complexity**: 2/10 (Simple)

## Objective
Remember and recite the longest chain of items without mistakes.

## Components
- 50 item cards (various objects, animals, colors)

## Setup
1. Shuffle cards and place face-down in center
2. Determine starting player

## Rules
1. First player flips one card and says its name
2. Next player flips a new card and says the first card's name, then the new card's name
3. Each subsequent player recites all previous cards in order, then adds a new card
4. If a player makes a mistake, they're eliminated
5. Reshuffle all cards and continue with remaining players

## Winning Condition
Last player remaining wins.

## Special Rules
- Players have 10 seconds to begin reciting the sequence
- Cards remain face-up during each round for verification
