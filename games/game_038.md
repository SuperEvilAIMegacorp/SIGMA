# Time Traders

## Game Information
- **Genre**: Trading & Economics
- **Players**: 3-5
- **Duration**: 40-50 minutes
- **Complexity**: 7/10 (Complex)

## Objective
Accumulate the most wealth by trading goods across time periods.

## Components
- 5 era boards (past to future)
- 60 commodity cards (6 types)
- Price tracker (fluctuates by era)
- Time machine tokens (3 per player)
- Starting money: 100 credits

## Setup
1. Arrange era boards chronologically
2. Set starting prices for each commodity in each era
3. Players start in "present" era
4. Shuffle commodity deck

## Rules
1. **Trading Phase:**
   - Buy/sell commodities at current era prices
   - Prices shown on price tracker
2. **Movement Phase:**
   - Use time machine token to move one era forward or backward
   - Tokens recharge after visiting all 5 eras (cycle complete)
3. **Market Phase:**
   - Roll dice to adjust prices in current era (random events)
4. **Reveal Phase:**
   - Reveal next commodity card (affects supply/demand)

## Winning Condition
After 12 rounds, player with most credits (cash + commodity values at current position) wins.

## Special Rules
- Commodities have different price curves across eras
- Can't sell commodities you don't have
- Limited cargo: hold max 10 commodities
- Some events create arbitrage opportunities
- Futures contracts: bet on price changes
