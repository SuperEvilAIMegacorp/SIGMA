# Echo Chamber

## Game Information
- **Genre**: Communication Game
- **Players**: 4-8 (even number)
- **Duration**: 20-30 minutes
- **Complexity**: 3/10 (Simple-Medium)

## Objective
Communicate messages through distortion and interpretation.

## Components
- 50 phrase cards
- Timer (60 seconds per round)
- Score tracker
- Interpretation sheets

## Setup
1. Form pairs of players
2. Shuffle phrase cards
3. Pairs sit back-to-back

## Rules
1. One player draws a phrase card (e.g., "Dancing elephants wear tutus")
2. They have 60 seconds to convey it to partner through:
   - Humming
   - Clapping rhythms
   - Drawing in air (partner can't see)
3. Partner interprets and writes their guess
4. Score points based on accuracy:
   - Exact phrase: 5 points
   - Correct meaning: 3 points
   - Partially correct: 1 point
5. Rotate pairs each round

## Winning Condition
Pair with most points after all players have been "sender" twice wins.

## Special Rules
- No talking or recognizable sounds
- Other pairs can guess too (bonus 1 point if correct)
- Some phrases have visual, auditory, or conceptual variants
