# Coin Flip Quest

## Game Information
- **Genre**: Push-Your-Luck
- **Players**: 2-5
- **Duration**: 10-15 minutes
- **Complexity**: 2/10 (Simple)

## Objective
Score exactly 20 points through successful coin flips.

## Components
- 1 coin
- Score sheet

## Setup
1. All players start at 0 points
2. Determine starting player

## Rules
1. On your turn, decide how many flips you want to attempt (1-5)
2. Call heads or tails before each flip
3. Each correct call = 1 point
4. First incorrect call ends your turn, and you lose all points earned that turn
5. If all flips are correct, add points to your total score

## Winning Condition
First player to reach exactly 20 points wins.

## Special Rules
- If you go over 20, reset to 15 points
- You must attempt at least 1 flip per turn
