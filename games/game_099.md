# Wavelength Warriors

## Game Information
- **Genre**: Team Communication
- **Players**: 4-12 (even number, teams of 2)
- **Duration**: 30-40 minutes
- **Complexity**: 3/10 (Simple-Medium)

## Objective
Work with your partner to communicate concepts on a spectrum using limited clues.

## Components
- Spectrum board (sliding scale 0-100)
- 200 spectrum cards (showing opposite extremes)
- Clue cards
- Target dial (hidden position)
- Score tracker
- Team markers

## Setup
1. Divide into teams of 2
2. Shuffle spectrum deck
3. Set score to 0 for all teams

## Rules
**Round Structure:**
1. **Setup Phase:**
   - Active team draws spectrum card (e.g., "Cold ← → Hot")
   - Secret dial shows target position (e.g., 37/100)
   - Clue-giver sees position, partner does not
2. **Clue Phase:**
   - Clue-giver provides ONE word/phrase clue
   - Clue should indicate where on spectrum target lies
   - Example: For 37/100 on "Cold → Hot", say "Cool autumn day"
3. **Guess Phase:**
   - Partner guesses position (0-100)
   - Place guess marker on spectrum
4. **Scoring:**
   - Reveal actual target position
   - Score based on accuracy:
     * Exact (±3): 4 points
     * Close (±10): 3 points
     * Near (±20): 2 points
     * Far (±30): 1 point
     * Miss (>30): 0 points
5. Rotate to next team

## Spectrum Categories
- **Physical:** Cold/Hot, Small/Large, Soft/Hard
- **Abstract:** Boring/Exciting, Scary/Comforting, Simple/Complex
- **Social:** Unpopular/Popular, Awkward/Smooth, Formal/Casual
- **Temporal:** Ancient/Modern, Fast/Slow, Brief/Eternal
- **Emotional:** Sad/Happy, Calm/Energetic, Mild/Intense

## Winning Condition
First team to 20 points wins.

## Special Rules
- **Calibration:**
  - Other teams can also guess (after main team)
  - Closest other team steals 1 point
- **Debate Phase:**
  - After scoring, 30-second discussion why placement makes sense
  - Great for laughs and seeing different perspectives
- **Challenge Mode:**
  - Teams can bet double points (double or nothing)
- **Wildcard Spectrums:**
  - Some cards are intentionally ambiguous
  - Subjective interpretations encouraged

## Strategic Elements
- Shared mental models
- Creative cluing
- Calibration to partner's thinking
- Cultural/personal reference understanding
- Avoiding obvious vs being too obscure

## Variants
- Solo mode: guess the average of all players
- 3-player teams: two partners guess, must agree
- Speed round: 10-second clue limit
- Custom spectrums: players create their own

## Social Dynamics
- Reveals how teams think similarly
- Creates funny misunderstandings
- Encourages creative communication
- Low-pressure party atmosphere

## Accessibility
- Simple core rules
- No prior knowledge needed
- Works for mixed groups
- Highly repeatable
