# Constellation Map

## Game Information
- **Genre**: Pattern Matching
- **Players**: 2-4
- **Duration**: 20-30 minutes
- **Complexity**: 4/10 (Medium)

## Objective
Create the most valuable star patterns by connecting dots.

## Components
- Star board (50 dot grid)
- Connection markers (different colors per player)
- 30 constellation cards (showing patterns to match)
- Score tracker

## Setup
1. Place star board in center
2. Shuffle constellation cards, reveal 5 face-up
3. Each player gets 15 connection markers

## Rules
1. On your turn:
   - Place 1-3 connection markers to link dots (orthogonal or diagonal)
   - Cannot cross other players' connections
   - Cannot reuse dots already connected
2. If your connections match a face-up constellation card:
   - Claim that card (score its points)
   - Reveal new card to replace it
3. Connections must be continuous (no gaps)

## Winning Condition
When all markers placed, player with most points wins.

## Special Rules
- Bonus: complete multiple constellations in one turn = +5 points
- Some constellations overlap (both players can claim partial credit)
- Master constellations (rare) worth 15 points, require 10+ connections
