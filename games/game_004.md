# Number Line

## Game Information
- **Genre**: Abstract Strategy
- **Players**: 2
- **Duration**: 5-10 minutes
- **Complexity**: 2/10 (Simple)

## Objective
Create a sequence of exactly 5 numbers in ascending order on your side.

## Components
- 30 numbered tiles (1-30, one of each)
- Game board with two rows of 15 spaces each

## Setup
1. Shuffle tiles face-down
2. Each player takes one row of the board
3. Draw pile placed in center

## Rules
1. On your turn, draw one tile
2. Place it in any empty space on your row
3. Once placed, tiles cannot be moved
4. Tiles must eventually form sequences reading left to right

## Winning Condition
First player to have 5 consecutive numbers in ascending order (not necessarily adjacent) wins.

## Special Rules
- Numbers don't need to be adjacent, but must be in left-to-right ascending order
- Example winning sequence: 3, 7, 15, 22, 28
