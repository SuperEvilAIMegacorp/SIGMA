# Storm Chasers

## Game Information
- **Genre**: Racing Game
- **Players**: 2-5
- **Duration**: 25-35 minutes
- **Complexity**: 5/10 (Medium)

## Objective
Navigate the track and reach the finish line while avoiding the storm.

## Components
- Modular track board (15 segments)
- Player vehicles
- Storm token (moves along track)
- Weather dice (custom: 1-3, storm symbol x2, lightning symbol x1)
- Hazard cards

## Setup
1. Arrange track segments randomly to form circuit
2. All players start on starting line
3. Place storm token at segment 1
4. Shuffle hazard deck

## Rules
1. On your turn:
   - Roll weather die
   - Move your vehicle forward that many spaces (storm = 0, lightning = draw hazard card)
   - After all players move, storm token advances 2 segments
2. If storm catches your vehicle, you're knocked back 3 spaces
3. Hazard cards create obstacles or penalties

## Winning Condition
First player to complete the full circuit wins.

## Special Rules
- You can't move through other players (must go around)
- Some track segments have special properties (shortcuts, slow zones)
- Lightning hazards can be kept and used strategically on other players
