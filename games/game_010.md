# Triple Dice

## Game Information
- **Genre**: Dice Game
- **Players**: 2-4
- **Duration**: 15-20 minutes
- **Complexity**: 2/10 (Simple)

## Objective
Collect the most sets of three matching dice results.

## Components
- 3 six-sided dice per player
- Score sheet

## Setup
1. Each player receives 3 dice
2. Determine starting player

## Rules
1. All players roll their 3 dice simultaneously
2. If you roll three matching numbers (triples), score 5 points
3. If you roll two matching numbers (doubles), keep those and re-roll the third die once
4. After all re-rolls, highest total score for that round gets 1 bonus point
5. Play 10 rounds

## Winning Condition
Player with most points after 10 rounds wins.

## Special Rules
- Ties for highest total: both players get bonus point
- Triple sixes = 10 points instead of 5
