# Constellation Codex

## Game Information
- **Genre**: Deck Building
- **Players**: 2-4
- **Duration**: 45-60 minutes
- **Complexity**: 7/10 (Complex)

## Objective
Build the most powerful deck of constellation cards to score points through card combinations.

## Components
- 120 constellation cards (12 constellations, 10 cards each)
- Starting decks (10 basic cards per player)
- Star tokens (currency)
- Cosmic board (market display)
- VP tracker

## Setup
1. Each player gets starting deck (shuffle, draw 5)
2. Place 8 constellation cards in market
3. Each player starts with 5 star tokens
4. Set VP to 0

## Rules
1. **Action Phase:** Take 1 action:
   - Play cards from hand (up to 5)
   - Buy 1 card from market (costs stars)
   - Activate constellation power (if complete)
2. **Resolution Phase:**
   - Cards generate: stars, draw power, VP, special effects
   - Check for constellation completion (all cards from one set)
3. **Cleanup Phase:**
   - Discard hand and played cards
   - Draw 5 new cards

## Constellation Mechanics
Each constellation has theme and synergy:
- **Orion (Hunter):** Attack/removal
- **Ursa Major (Bear):** Defense/resilience
- **Cassiopeia (Queen):** Draw/cycling
- **Phoenix:** Rebirth (recursion from discard)

## Winning Condition
First to 60 VP OR complete 3 full constellations.

## Special Rules
- Card synergies: playing multiple cards from same constellation triggers bonuses
- Cosmic events: every 3 rounds, random event affects all players
- Constellation powers: unique ultimate abilities when set complete
- Thin deck strategy vs fat deck strategy (balance)
- Some cards have Eclipse effects (trigger when discarded)

## Strategic Depth
- Deck composition
- Engine building
- Tempo vs value
- Constellation specialization vs diversification
