# Spell Duel

## Game Information
- **Genre**: Card Battler
- **Players**: 2
- **Duration**: 20-30 minutes
- **Complexity**: 6/10 (Medium-Complex)

## Objective
Reduce opponent's life points to 0 using spell cards.

## Components
- 40 spell cards per player (custom deck)
- Life counter (start at 30 each)
- Mana tracker (increases each turn)
- Spell reference sheet

## Setup
1. Each player shuffles their deck
2. Draw starting hand of 5 cards
3. Both start at 30 life, 1 mana
4. Randomly determine first player

## Rules
1. Start of turn: Gain 1 max mana, draw 1 card
2. Main phase:
   - Play spells by paying mana cost
   - Attack spells deal damage
   - Defense spells block damage (this turn only)
   - Utility spells have various effects
3. End phase: Mana refills to max
4. Max hand size: 7 (discard excess)

## Winning Condition
Reduce opponent to 0 life.

## Special Rules
- Can play multiple spells per turn if mana allows
- Some spells chain together (combo bonuses)
- Counter spells can negate opponent's spells
- Spell types: Attack (5 types), Defense (3 types), Utility (4 types)

## Spell Categories
- Fire: high damage, no effects
- Ice: moderate damage, freeze (skip turn effects)
- Lightning: low damage, chains to multiple targets
- Healing: restore life
- Shield: prevent damage
