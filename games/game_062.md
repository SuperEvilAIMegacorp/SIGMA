# Tribal Council

## Game Information
- **Genre**: Negotiation & Alliance
- **Players**: 5-8
- **Duration**: 45-60 minutes
- **Complexity**: 6/10 (Medium-Complex)

## Objective
Survive elimination votes and accumulate the most influence.

## Components
- Voting tokens
- Alliance cards
- Immunity challenges (mini-games)
- Influence tracker
- Secret advantage tokens

## Setup
1. Each player starts with 5 influence points
2. Shuffle alliance cards
3. Prepare immunity challenge deck
4. Randomly distribute 2 secret advantages

## Rules
**Round Structure:**
1. **Challenge Phase:** Complete immunity challenge (varies)
   - Winner gains immunity from elimination this round
   - Second place chooses one player to gain +1 influence
2. **Alliance Phase:** Form temporary alliances (max 3 players)
   - Discuss strategy (5 minutes)
   - Can break alliances anytime
3. **Voting Phase:** All players secretly vote to eliminate someone
   - Immune player can't be voted out
   - Most votes = eliminated
4. **Resolution:** Eliminated player loses 3 influence (if ≤0, out of game)

## Winning Condition
Last player standing OR most influence after 10 rounds.

## Special Rules
- Tied votes: revote between tied players
- Secret advantages:
  - Extra vote
  - Force revote
  - Immunity steal
- Alliance betrayal: bonus +2 influence to betrayer
- Can spend 3 influence for immunity (once per game)
