# Voting Bloc

## Game Information
- **Genre**: Social Deduction
- **Players**: 5-10
- **Duration**: 30-40 minutes
- **Complexity**: 6/10 (Medium-Complex)

## Objective
Achieve your secret objective through strategic voting and alliances.

## Components
- Role cards (each with hidden objective)
- 10 issue cards
- Voting tokens (Yes/No per player)
- Influence tokens
- Score tracker

## Setup
1. Each player draws a secret role card
2. Shuffle issue deck
3. Each player gets 3 influence tokens
4. Roles include: Loyalist, Rebel, Profiteer, Chaos Agent, Swing Vote

## Rules
1. Reveal one issue card
2. **Discussion Phase:** Players debate (2 minutes) - can't reveal roles
3. **Voting Phase:** All players vote Yes or No simultaneously
4. **Resolution Phase:**
   - Outcome determined by majority
   - Players score based on how outcome affects their role objective
5. Can spend influence tokens to:
   - Vote twice
   - Force revote
   - Block someone's vote

## Winning Condition
After 10 issues, player who best achieved their role objective wins (points tracked secretly).

## Special Rules
- Roles want different outcomes (some want chaos, others consistency)
- Can form temporary alliances
- Profiteer scores when votes are close (regardless of outcome)
- Chaos Agent scores when unexpected votes occur
