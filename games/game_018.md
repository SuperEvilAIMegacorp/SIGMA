# Market Day

## Game Information
- **Genre**: Trading Game
- **Players**: 3-5
- **Duration**: 30-40 minutes
- **Complexity**: 4/10 (Medium)

## Objective
Fulfill the most market orders by trading and collecting goods.

## Components
- 5 types of goods (40 cards each): grain, fish, cloth, spices, tools
- 30 order cards (various combinations required)
- Starting hand: 8 random good cards per player

## Setup
1. Shuffle goods deck
2. Deal 8 cards to each player
3. Reveal 5 order cards face-up
4. Place goods deck as draw pile

## Rules
1. On your turn, take 2 actions from:
   - Draw 2 goods cards
   - Trade goods with another player (voluntary for both)
   - Fulfill 1 order card (discard required goods, take order card)
   - Refresh orders: discard all face-up orders and reveal 5 new ones (once per game)

## Winning Condition
First player to fulfill 5 order cards wins.

## Special Rules
- Hand limit: 12 cards (discard down at end of turn)
- Some order cards have bonus points for multiple completions
- When orders deck empty, reshuffle discarded orders
