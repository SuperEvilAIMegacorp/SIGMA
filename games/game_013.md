# Shadow Maze

## Game Information
- **Genre**: Deduction Game
- **Players**: 3-5
- **Duration**: 25-35 minutes
- **Complexity**: 6/10 (Medium-Complex)

## Objective
Navigate the invisible maze and reach the goal while deducing the maze layout.

## Components
- 7x7 grid board
- Player pawns
- Maze master screen
- Maze layout sheet (hidden)
- Movement log

## Setup
1. Maze master creates a secret maze layout with walls
2. All players start at designated start space
3. Goal space is revealed

## Rules
1. On your turn, declare a direction to move (up, down, left, right)
2. Maze master says "allowed" or "blocked"
3. If allowed, move your pawn; if blocked, stay put
4. All movements are visible to other players
5. Players can deduce wall locations from others' movements

## Winning Condition
First player to reach the goal space wins.

## Special Rules
- You can pass your turn to observe others
- Maze master must be consistent - same move from same space always gives same result
- Players can take notes about wall locations
