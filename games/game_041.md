# River Crossing

## Game Information
- **Genre**: Puzzle/Logic Game
- **Players**: 1-2 (competitive or puzzle-solve)
- **Duration**: 15-25 minutes
- **Complexity**: 5/10 (Medium)

## Objective
Transport all pieces across the river following constraint rules.

## Components
- River board (2 banks connected by boat space)
- 8 piece tokens (4 types: farmers, wolves, sheep, cabbages)
- Boat token (capacity 2)
- Rule reference card
- Move counter

## Setup
1. Place all pieces on left bank
2. Boat starts on left bank
3. Goal: get all pieces to right bank

## Rules
**Constraints:**
- Boat holds max 2 pieces (or 1 piece + rower)
- Wolves can't be left alone with sheep (sheep eaten)
- Sheep can't be left alone with cabbages (cabbages eaten)
- Someone must row the boat (piece or dedicated rower)

**Competitive Mode (2 players):**
- Players alternate moves
- First to successfully transfer all pieces in fewest moves wins

**Solo Puzzle Mode:**
- Find minimum number of moves (optimal: 11 moves)

## Winning Condition
**Competitive:** Lowest move count
**Solo:** Beat par (15 moves)

## Special Rules
- Any violation of constraints = reset to start
- Advanced: add time limits per move
- Variant: different piece combinations and constraints
