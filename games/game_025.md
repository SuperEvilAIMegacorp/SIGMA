# Midnight Market

## Game Information
- **Genre**: Bluffing & Negotiation
- **Players**: 3-6
- **Duration**: 30-40 minutes
- **Complexity**: 5/10 (Medium)

## Objective
Accumulate the most wealth by trading goods, but beware of counterfeit items.

## Components
- 80 goods cards (authentic and counterfeit versions)
- 30 inspection tokens
- Starting money: 50 coins per player
- Reputation tracker

## Setup
1. Shuffle goods deck (mix of authentic and counterfeit)
2. Deal 5 cards to each player
3. Each player starts with 3 inspection tokens
4. Set reputation to 5 for all players

## Rules
1. On your turn, offer to sell 1-3 goods cards to another player
2. Name your price and claim they're authentic
3. Buyer can:
   - Accept: pay price, take cards
   - Inspect: use token, reveal truth
     * If counterfeit: seller loses 2 reputation, buyer gets cards free
     * If authentic: buyer must pay double price
   - Decline: turn passes
4. Draw back up to 5 cards at end of turn

## Winning Condition
After 12 rounds, most money wins. Reputation below 0 = disqualified.

## Special Rules
- Authentic goods have variable values (revealed at game end)
- Counterfeits = -5 coins
- At 0 inspection tokens, you must accept or decline (no inspection)
