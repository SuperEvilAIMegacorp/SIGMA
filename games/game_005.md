# Quick Draw

## Game Information
- **Genre**: Party Game
- **Players**: 3-8
- **Duration**: 15-20 minutes
- **Complexity**: 1/10 (Simple)

## Objective
Score the most points by guessing drawings correctly.

## Components
- 100 word cards
- Drawing paper
- Pencils
- 60-second timer

## Setup
1. Place word cards face-down in center
2. Give each player paper and pencil
3. Determine starting player

## Rules
1. Active player draws a card and has 60 seconds to draw it
2. Other players call out guesses
3. First player to guess correctly scores 2 points
4. Drawer scores 1 point if anyone guesses correctly
5. Rotate drawer clockwise

## Winning Condition
First player to reach 10 points wins.

## Special Rules
- No letters, numbers, or speaking allowed while drawing
- If time runs out with no correct guess, no one scores
