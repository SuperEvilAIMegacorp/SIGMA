# Story Forge

## Game Information
- **Genre**: Collaborative Storytelling
- **Players**: 3-6
- **Duration**: 30-40 minutes
- **Complexity**: 3/10 (Simple-Medium)

## Objective
Create the most compelling story collaboratively while pursuing secret objectives.

## Components
- Story element cards (characters, settings, objects, events)
- Secret objective cards (what you want in the story)
- Story tokens (track contributions)
- Voting tokens

## Setup
1. Each player draws 1 character, 1 setting, 1 object card
2. Each player draws 1 secret objective (e.g., "make the story end sadly")
3. Randomly determine starting narrator

## Rules
1. **Narration Round:**
   - Active narrator tells story for 2 minutes
   - Must incorporate cards they hold
   - Other players can challenge if cards not used
2. **Contribution Phase:**
   - Other players add one story element (30 seconds each)
   - Must build on narrator's story logically
3. **Integration Phase:**
   - Narrator weaves contributions into story (1 minute)
4. **Rotation:** Next player becomes narrator
5. After all players have narrated, vote on best story

## Winning Condition
Points awarded for:
- Winning story vote: 5 points
- Secret objective achieved: 3 points
- Story tokens collected: 1 point each

## Special Rules
- Story tokens earned by:
  - Clever card integration
  - Plot twists
  - Emotional moments
- Can trade cards between players
- Veto power: majority can veto illogical additions
