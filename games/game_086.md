# Asteroid Mining

## Game Information
- **Genre**: Resource Management
- **Players**: 2-4
- **Duration**: 45-60 minutes
- **Complexity**: 7/10 (Complex)

## Objective
Extract the most valuable resources from the asteroid field while managing your mining fleet.

## Components
- Asteroid field board (hexagonal grid with 50+ asteroids)
- Mining ship meeples (3 per player)
- Resource tokens (5 types: iron, gold, platinum, crystals, fuel)
- Cargo capacity trackers
- Market price board (fluctuating)
- Equipment upgrade cards

## Setup
1. Randomly place asteroid tokens (each has hidden resources)
2. Each player starts with 3 mining ships at space station
3. Initial money: 100 credits
4. Set starting market prices

## Rules
**Turn Structure:**
1. **Action Phase:** Each ship can take one action:
   - **Move:** Travel to adjacent hex (costs fuel)
   - **Scan:** Reveal asteroid resources
   - **Mine:** Extract resources (takes 1-3 turns based on asteroid size)
   - **Return:** Deliver cargo to station
   - **Upgrade:** Buy better equipment at station
2. **Market Phase:**
   - Sell resources at current prices
   - Prices fluctuate based on supply/demand
3. **Maintenance Phase:**
   - Pay ship upkeep (2 credits per ship)
   - Refuel ships

## Winning Condition
Most credits after 15 rounds OR first to 500 credits.

## Special Rules
- **Cargo Limits:**
  - Basic ship: 5 units
  - Upgraded: 10-20 units
- **Asteroid Types:**
  - Common (iron): abundant, low value
  - Rare (gold/platinum): scarce, high value
  - Crystal asteroids: very valuable but dangerous to mine
  - Fuel asteroids: necessary for operations
- **Mining Efficiency:**
  - Better equipment = faster extraction
  - Some asteroids require specific tools
- **Market Dynamics:**
  - Flooding market with one resource drops price
  - Strategic selling timing crucial
- **Hazards:**
  - Solar flares (random event, ships take damage)
  - Collisions (overcrowded areas)
  - Fuel depletion (stranded ships)

## Strategic Elements
- Fleet positioning
- Resource speculation
- Upgrade investment timing
- Market manipulation
- Risk management
