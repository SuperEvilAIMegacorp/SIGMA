# Dungeon Delve

## Game Information
- **Genre**: Dungeon Crawler (Card-based)
- **Players**: 1-4 (cooperative)
- **Duration**: 45-60 minutes
- **Complexity**: 7/10 (Complex)

## Objective
Work together to explore the dungeon, defeat monsters, and reach the treasure room.

## Components
- 60 dungeon room cards (rooms, monsters, treasures, traps)
- 4 hero cards (different abilities)
- Item cards (40)
- Health/stamina trackers
- Dice (combat: d6, d8, d10, d12)

## Setup
1. Each player chooses a hero (20 HP, 10 stamina)
2. Shuffle dungeon deck
3. Place entrance card
4. Each hero draws 2 starting items

## Rules
1. **Exploration Phase:** Reveal next room card
2. **Encounter Phase:** Resolve room effect:
   - **Monster:** Combat (roll hero die vs monster die, compare)
   - **Trap:** Make stamina check or take damage
   - **Treasure:** Draw item cards
   - **Empty:** Rest (recover 2 stamina)
3. **Rest Phase:** Use items, trade with other heroes
4. Proceed to next room

**Combat:**
- Roll your hero die + weapon modifier
- Monster rolls its die
- Higher result wins (deal damage equal to difference)
- Ties = both take 1 damage

## Winning Condition
Reach and clear the treasure room (boss fight) with at least one hero alive.

## Special Rules
- Dead heroes can be revived at rest points (costs 5 stamina from living hero)
- Item synergies provide bonuses
- Boss has 50 HP and special mechanics
