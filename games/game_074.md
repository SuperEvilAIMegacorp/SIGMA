# Summit Race

## Game Information
- **Genre**: Racing/Climbing Game
- **Players**: 2-5
- **Duration**: 30-40 minutes
- **Complexity**: 5/10 (Medium)

## Objective
Be the first to reach the mountain summit while managing stamina and weather.

## Components
- Mountain board (vertical track with 30 spaces)
- Climber meeples
- Weather dice
- Stamina trackers (start at 20)
- Equipment cards (40)
- Hazard tokens

## Setup
1. Place all climbers at base camp (space 0)
2. Randomly place 10 hazard tokens on mountain
3. Each player draws 3 equipment cards
4. Shuffle weather deck

## Rules
1. **Planning Phase:**
   - Declare how many spaces you'll climb (1-5)
   - More spaces = more stamina cost
2. **Climbing Phase:**
   - Pay stamina (1 per space, +1 for each hazard passed)
   - Move climber
3. **Weather Phase:**
   - Roll weather dice
   - Bad weather: all climbers lose extra stamina or slide down
4. **Rest Phase:**
   - Can choose to rest (recover 3 stamina, don't move)
   - Play equipment cards for benefits

## Winning Condition
First to reach summit (space 30) OR highest position when someone runs out of stamina.

## Special Rules
- Altitude zones: spaces 1-10 (low), 11-20 (medium), 21-30 (death zone)
- Death zone: double stamina costs
- Equipment examples:
  - Oxygen tank: reduce death zone cost
  - Rope: reroll weather dice
  - Tent: bonus rest recovery
- Push too hard = risk exhaustion
- Can help other players (stamina sharing)

## Hazards
- Crevasse: skip turn
- Avalanche path: weather-dependent damage
- Ice wall: requires equipment to pass
