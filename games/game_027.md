# Speed Stack

## Game Information
- **Genre**: Real-Time Card Game
- **Players**: 2-6
- **Duration**: 10-15 minutes
- **Complexity**: 2/10 (Simple)

## Objective
Get rid of all your cards first by playing them in ascending or descending sequence.

## Components
- 100 number cards (1-25, four copies of each)
- Timer

## Setup
1. Shuffle deck
2. Deal entire deck evenly to all players
3. Players hold cards face-down as personal draw pile

## Rules
1. When timer starts, all players play simultaneously
2. Four foundation piles start in center (beginning with any card)
3. Play cards in ascending OR descending sequence by 1
   - Example: 7→8→9 or 7→6→5
4. Can change direction on any foundation pile
5. No turns—first to play goes

## Winning Condition
First player to play all their cards wins.

## Special Rules
- Can play on any of the 4 foundation piles
- Number 1 and 25 connect (25→1 or 1→25 allowed)
- No hand—flip cards one at a time from your draw pile
