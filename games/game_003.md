# Tower of Cards

## Game Information
- **Genre**: Dexterity Game
- **Players**: 2-5
- **Duration**: 10-20 minutes
- **Complexity**: 1/10 (Simple)

## Objective
Build the tallest tower without knocking it over.

## Components
- Standard 52-card deck

## Setup
1. Clear a flat, stable playing surface
2. Determine starting player

## Rules
1. On your turn, add one or two cards to the tower structure
2. Cards can be placed flat, leaning, or as roofs
3. You must use at least one hand to place cards, no tools
4. If the tower falls during your turn, you are eliminated
5. Play continues clockwise

## Winning Condition
Last player remaining after all others have been eliminated wins.

## Special Rules
- If a card falls but the tower stands, pick up the fallen card and your turn ends
- You have 30 seconds to place your card(s)
