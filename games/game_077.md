# Artifact Assembly

## Game Information
- **Genre**: Spatial Puzzle
- **Players**: 1-4
- **Duration**: 20-30 minutes
- **Complexity**: 5/10 (Medium)

## Objective
Assemble artifact pieces into complete objects following blueprint constraints.

## Components
- 60 artifact pieces (various shapes: tetromino-like)
- 30 blueprint cards (showing target shapes)
- Assembly boards
- Timer (3 minutes per puzzle)
- Difficulty tracker

## Setup
1. Shuffle blueprint deck
2. Each player gets assembly board
3. Pool artifact pieces in center
4. Competitive mode: each player races independently
5. Cooperative mode: team works on one large artifact

## Rules
**Competitive Mode:**
1. Reveal blueprint card
2. All players race to grab pieces and assemble
3. First to complete correctly wins round (3 points)
4. Others can still complete for 1 point

**Cooperative Mode:**
1. Team assembles large artifact together
2. More complex blueprints
3. Must discuss strategy
4. Beat par time for difficulty level

## Winning Condition
**Competitive:** First to 15 points
**Cooperative:** Complete 5 artifacts under par time

## Special Rules
- Pieces can't overlap
- Must fit exactly on blueprint grid
- Rotation allowed, flipping not allowed (unless blueprint specifies)
- Special pieces:
  - Wildcard: fits anywhere (1 per puzzle)
  - Locked: must be placed first
  - Magnetic: attaches to specific other pieces
- Time bonus: finish 30+ seconds early = +1 point

## Difficulty Levels
1. Simple: 6-8 pieces, obvious fits
2. Medium: 10-12 pieces, some trial needed
3. Hard: 15-20 pieces, tight constraints
4. Expert: 25+ pieces, 3D assembly
