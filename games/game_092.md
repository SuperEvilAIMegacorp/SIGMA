# Artifact Auction

## Game Information
- **Genre**: Auction/Set Collection
- **Players**: 3-6
- **Duration**: 40-50 minutes
- **Complexity**: 6/10 (Medium-Complex)

## Objective
Acquire the most valuable collection of historical artifacts through strategic bidding.

## Components
- 80 artifact cards (8 historical periods, 10 artifacts each)
- 100 coins per player (starting money)
- Auction board (4 auction types)
- Expert consultant cards (special abilities)
- Provenance tokens (authenticity markers)
- Museum display boards

## Setup
1. Each player gets 100 coins and display board
2. Shuffle artifact deck
3. Shuffle consultant cards, deal 2 per player
4. Place provenance tokens

## Rules
**Round Structure:**
1. **Reveal Phase:**
   - Reveal 4 artifacts
   - Place on different auction spaces (each has different auction type)
2. **Auction Phase:**
   - Conduct auctions (types below)
   - Winners pay bid, take artifact
3. **Display Phase:**
   - Add artifacts to museum display
   - Score complete sets/exhibits

## Auction Types
1. **Open Auction:** Classic ascending bid
2. **Sealed Bid:** Simultaneous secret bids
3. **Dutch Auction:** Descending price, first to claim wins
4. **All-Pay:** Everyone pays their bid, highest wins artifact

## Scoring
- **Period Sets:** All 10 artifacts from one period: 50 points
- **Theme Sets:** 5 artifacts with common theme: 20 points
- **Rarity Bonus:** Unique artifacts: 5-15 points each
- **Provenance:** Verified authenticity: +5 points per artifact

## Winning Condition
Most points after all artifacts auctioned (20 rounds).

## Special Rules
- **Expert Consultants:**
  - Use once per game for special ability
  - Examples:
    * See hidden auction card early
    * Bid with -20% discount
    * Force re-auction
    * Swap one artifact with opponent
- **Provenance Tokens:**
  - Limited tokens verify artifact authenticity
  - Some artifacts are forgeries (revealed at game end, worth 0)
  - Must decide when to verify vs risk it
- **Loans:**
  - Borrow up to 50 coins (pay back 60 at game end)
- **Museum Effects:**
  - Some artifact combinations unlock special bonuses
  - Example: Egyptian crown + scepter = +10 bonus points

## Strategic Complexity
- Budget management
- Set speculation
- Auction type advantage
- Forgery risk assessment
- Consultant timing
- Opponent blocking
