# Dice Race

## Game Information
- **Genre**: Dice Game
- **Players**: 2-6
- **Duration**: 5-10 minutes
- **Complexity**: 1/10 (Simple)

## Objective
Be the first player to reach exactly 50 points by rolling dice.

## Components
- 2 standard six-sided dice
- Score sheet and pencil

## Setup
1. Each player starts at 0 points
2. Determine starting player randomly

## Rules
1. On your turn, roll both dice
2. Add the sum to your total score
3. If you roll doubles, roll again and add that sum too (can chain multiple times)
4. Play passes to the next player

## Winning Condition
First player to reach exactly 50 points wins. If you go over 50, subtract that roll and your turn ends.

## Special Rules
- Rolling double sixes subtracts 5 points from your score (before adding the 12)
