# Symbiosis

## Game Information
- **Genre**: Cooperative Evolution
- **Players**: 2-4
- **Duration**: 40-50 minutes
- **Complexity**: 6/10 (Medium-Complex)

## Objective
Work together to evolve interconnected species that survive environmental challenges.

## Components
- Species boards (one per player)
- Trait cards (80: various evolutionary traits)
- Environment cards (30: changing conditions)
- Food tokens
- Population trackers
- Extinction tracker

## Setup
1. Each player creates a starting species (basic traits)
2. Shuffle environment deck
3. Pool food tokens
4. Extinction tracker starts at 0

## Rules
1. **Environment Phase:**
   - Reveal environment card (drought, ice age, new predator, etc.)
   - Changes food availability and survival requirements
2. **Evolution Phase:**
   - Players draft trait cards
   - Add traits to species (max 5 per species)
   - Traits cost food tokens
3. **Feeding Phase:**
   - Species eat based on traits
   - Not enough food = population decline
4. **Survival Check:**
   - Species must meet environment requirements
   - Failed check: +1 extinction
   - At 5 extinctions, team loses

## Winning Condition
Survive 10 environment cards with thriving populations.

## Special Rules
- Symbiotic traits: species can help each other
  - Mutualism: both benefit
  - Commensalism: one benefits, one neutral
  - Parasitism: one benefits, one harmed (can harm opponents)
- Trait combinations create emergent strategies
- Example traits:
  - Carnivore (eat other species)
  - Nocturnal (avoid daytime threats)
  - Cooperation (share food with allies)
  - Camouflage (avoid predators)
  - Burrowing (survive drought)

## Strategic Complexity
- Anticipate environment changes
- Build complementary species
- Resource sharing decisions
- Short-term survival vs long-term adaptation
