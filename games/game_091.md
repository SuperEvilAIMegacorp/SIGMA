# Resonance Chamber

## Game Information
- **Genre**: Audio/Pattern Game
- **Players**: 3-6
- **Duration**: 25-30 minutes
- **Complexity**: 3/10 (Simple-Medium)

## Objective
Create harmonious sound patterns and score points through audio matching.

## Components
- 80 sound cards (showing waveforms/frequencies)
- Harmonic reference chart
- Dissonance tracker
- Composition boards
- Score tokens

## Setup
1. Shuffle sound deck
2. Each player draws 7 sound cards
3. Dissonance tracker starts at 0
4. Reference chart visible to all

## Rules
1. On your turn:
   - Play 1-3 sound cards to your composition
   - Cards combine based on harmonic relationships:
     * Perfect harmony (octaves, fifths): +3 points
     * Pleasant (thirds, sixths): +2 points
     * Neutral: +1 point
     * Dissonant (tritones, seconds): -2 points, +1 dissonance
2. When you have 5+ cards in composition:
   - Score it (add up all relationships)
   - Clear composition, start new one
3. Draw back to 7 cards

## Winning Condition
First to 30 points OR highest score when dissonance reaches 20 (game ends, everyone loses).

## Special Rules
- **Harmonic Series:**
  - Building on fundamental frequency multiplies score
  - Root → Fifth → Octave chain: 2x multiplier
- **Dissonance Management:**
  - Cooperative element: high dissonance affects all players
  - Can spend turn to reduce dissonance by 3 (community service)
- **Special Cards:**
  - Wild harmonics: match any frequency
  - Silence cards: reset dissonance
  - Amplifier cards: double next card's score
- **Complex Chords:**
  - 4+ harmonious cards simultaneously: bonus +5 points

## Strategic Elements
- Balance point chasing vs dissonance control
- Hand management
- Timing amplifiers
- Cooperative vs competitive tension

## Educational Aspect
- Learn about musical intervals
- Understand frequency relationships
- Pattern recognition
- Risk vs reward in building compositions

## Variants
- Team mode: pairs work together
- Real sounds: use apps to play actual tones
- Expert mode: add rhythm and timing elements
