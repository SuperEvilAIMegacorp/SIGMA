# Cascade Kingdom

## Game Information
- **Genre**: Puzzle/Territory Building
- **Players**: 2-4
- **Duration**: 35-45 minutes
- **Complexity**: 6/10 (Medium-Complex)

## Objective
Build the most valuable cascading water system through your kingdom.

## Components
- Modular terrain boards (mountains, hills, valleys)
- Water source tokens (springs)
- Channel pieces (aqueducts, rivers, waterfalls)
- Mill/building tokens (score points from water flow)
- Elevation markers
- Flow calculation board

## Setup
1. Assemble terrain (3D landscape with varying elevations)
2. Place 4 water sources at highest elevations
3. Each player gets channel pieces and buildings
4. Randomly determine turn order

## Rules
1. **Building Phase:**
   - Place one channel piece on terrain
   - Must connect to water source or existing channel
   - Water flows downhill (follow elevation rules)
2. **Flow Phase:**
   - Trace water path from sources
   - Water flows through your channels based on gravity
   - Buildings along flow path generate resources
3. **Construction Phase:**
   - Spend resources to build mills, dams, reservoirs
   - These multiply your point generation

## Winning Condition
Most points after all channel pieces placed (approximately 15 rounds).

## Special Rules
- **Elevation Requirements:**
  - Water only flows downhill
  - Must respect height differences
  - Can use aqueducts to cross valleys
- **Building Types:**
  - **Mills:** Generate points per unit of water flow
  - **Dams:** Store water, release later for burst
  - **Reservoirs:** Increase flow capacity
  - **Gardens:** Bonus if water reaches them
- **Flow Mechanics:**
  - Limited water per source (distribute among all channels)
  - Can split flow (reduce pressure in each branch)
  - Merging flows combines volumes
- **Interaction:**
  - Can connect to opponent channels
  - Diverting their water is strategic
  - Building dams affects downstream players

## Scoring
- Each building scores based on water volume reaching it
- Complete water networks (source → sea): +10 bonus
- Longest continuous channel: +5 bonus
- Most efficient system (fewest pieces): +5 bonus

## Strategic Elements
- Optimal path planning (shortest downhill routes)
- Network efficiency vs redundancy
- Building placement timing
- Water source competition
- Cooperative vs competitive flow sharing

## Complexity Factors
- 3D spatial reasoning
- Flow calculation
- Resource generation curves
- Multi-path optimization
