# Pocket Kingdoms

## Game Information
- **Genre**: Micro Strategy
- **Players**: 2
- **Duration**: 10-15 minutes
- **Complexity**: 4/10 (Medium)

## Objective
Control more territory cards than your opponent.

## Components
- 18 territory cards (numbered 1-9, two copies)
- 6 action cards per player

## Setup
1. Shuffle territory cards face-down in 3x6 grid
2. Each player gets their 6 action cards

## Rules
1. Players alternate turns
2. On your turn:
   - Play one action card face-down
   - Both players reveal simultaneously
   - Higher number wins the territory
   - Place winner's marker on that territory
3. Tied number: both players discard action, territory remains unclaimed
4. Action cards can't be reused

## Winning Condition
After all territories claimed, player with most territories wins.

## Special Rules
- Some territories have special powers when controlled
- Action cards: 1, 2, 2, 3, 3, 4 (asymmetric betting)
- Crown territories (corners) worth 2 each
