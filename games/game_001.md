# Color Match

## Game Information
- **Genre**: Card Game
- **Players**: 2-4
- **Duration**: 10-15 minutes
- **Complexity**: 1/10 (Simple)

## Objective
Be the first player to get rid of all your cards by matching colors or numbers.

## Components
- 60 cards: 4 colors (red, blue, green, yellow), numbers 1-15 in each color

## Setup
1. Shuffle the deck
2. Deal 5 cards to each player
3. Place remaining cards face-down as draw pile
4. Flip top card to start discard pile

## Rules
1. On your turn, play one card that matches either the color OR number of the top discard card
2. If you cannot play, draw one card from the draw pile
3. If the drawn card can be played, you may play it immediately
4. Play passes clockwise

## Winning Condition
First player to play all their cards wins.

## Special Rules
- If draw pile is empty, shuffle discard pile (except top card) to form new draw pile
