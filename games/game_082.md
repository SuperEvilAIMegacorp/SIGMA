# Chain Reaction

## Game Information
- **Genre**: Abstract Strategy
- **Players**: 2-3
- **Duration**: 15-20 minutes
- **Complexity**: 6/10 (Medium-Complex)

## Objective
Trigger the largest chain reaction to capture opponent's pieces.

## Components
- 6x6 grid board
- Energy tokens (3 colors, 30 each)
- Critical mass markers
- Reaction tracker

## Setup
1. Empty board
2. Each player takes energy tokens in their color
3. Randomly determine first player

## Rules
1. On your turn, place 1 energy token on any space
2. Each space has critical mass (based on position):
   - Corners: 2 tokens
   - Edges: 3 tokens
   - Center: 4 tokens
3. When a space reaches critical mass:
   - **Explodes:** Remove all tokens from that space
   - **Chain:** Place 1 token on each adjacent space (orthogonal)
   - **Conversion:** All tokens become your color
4. Chains can trigger more explosions (cascading)

## Winning Condition
Last player with tokens on board OR most tokens after 20 turns.

## Special Rules
- Explosions are mandatory (no choice)
- Chain reactions can spread across entire board
- Strategic placement can trigger massive combos
- Opponent's spaces can be hijacked via chains
- Can sacrifice pieces to set up future chains

## Strategic Elements
- Critical mass management
- Board control vs chain setup
- Defensive positioning
- Calculating cascade effects
- Timing (when to trigger vs when to build)

## Advanced Rules
- Variable critical masses (randomized at start)
- Neutron spaces (require +1 to critical mass)
- Shields (protect from one explosion)
- Multiplier spaces (double chain effect)

## Example
Turn 1: Place token on corner (1/2)
Turn 2: Place second token on same corner → EXPLODES
- Tokens go to adjacent spaces
- If those spaces reach critical mass, they explode too
- Chain continues until stable
