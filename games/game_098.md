# Ecosystem Engineers

## Game Information
- **Genre**: Tableau Building
- **Players**: 2-4
- **Duration**: 50-60 minutes
- **Complexity**: 7/10 (Complex)

## Objective
Build the most balanced and valuable ecosystem by placing species cards strategically.

## Components
- 120 species cards (plants, herbivores, carnivores, decomposers)
- Ecosystem boards (per player)
- Energy tokens (sun energy)
- Food chain markers
- Biodiversity tracker
- Event cards (environmental changes)

## Setup
1. Each player gets ecosystem board (3x4 grid)
2. Shuffle species deck by category
3. Each player draws 5 cards
4. Pool energy tokens
5. Set biodiversity to 0

## Rules
**Turn Structure:**
1. **Sun Phase:**
   - Collect energy equal to plant count
2. **Play Phase:**
   - Play 1 species card (costs energy based on trophic level)
   - Place on your ecosystem grid
   - Must meet placement requirements
3. **Food Chain Phase:**
   - Resolve predator-prey relationships
   - Species feed or die
4. **Evolution Phase:**
   - Draw 2 cards
   - Can upgrade existing species (pay energy)

## Species Requirements
- **Plants:** Need sun exposure (edge spaces or open spaces)
- **Herbivores:** Must be adjacent to plants
- **Carnivores:** Must be adjacent to herbivores
- **Decomposers:** Benefit from dead species

## Winning Condition
Most points after 12 rounds. Points from:
- Species diversity (different types): 2 points each
- Complete food chains: 10 points
- Balanced ecosystem (all categories): 15 bonus
- Energy efficiency: 1 point per 5 unused energy

## Special Rules
- **Symbiosis:**
  - Some species have synergies
  - Bee + Flower = double energy
  - Cleaner fish + Shark = both protected
- **Predation:**
  - Carnivores need prey or die (removed)
  - Overpredation: too many carnivores destabilize ecosystem
- **Carrying Capacity:**
  - Grid limits population
  - Must balance population sizes
- **Environmental Events:**
  - Drought: plants require double energy
  - Overpopulation: lose species
  - Mutation: upgrade species free
- **Extinction Cascades:**
  - Removing keystone species affects others
  - Can trigger collapse of food chain

## Species Card Elements
Each card shows:
- Energy cost
- Food requirements
- Predators/prey
- Special abilities
- Point value

## Strategic Depth
- Food web architecture
- Energy economy
- Species placement optimization
- Adapting to events
- Risk management (unstable vs safe ecosystems)
- Combo building (symbiotic pairs)

## Educational Value
- Ecology concepts
- Food webs
- Energy transfer
- Population dynamics
- Biodiversity importance

## Complexity Factors
- Multiple resource types
- Interconnected systems
- Cascading effects
- Long-term planning
- Adaptive strategy
