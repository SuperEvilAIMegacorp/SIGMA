# Gravity Shift

## Game Information
- **Genre**: Abstract Strategy
- **Players**: 2
- **Duration**: 15-20 minutes
- **Complexity**: 5/10 (Medium)

## Objective
Align 4 of your pieces in a row while the board rotates.

## Components
- 6x6 grid board (rotatable platform)
- 18 black pieces
- 18 white pieces
- Gravity die (showing N, S, E, W)

## Setup
1. Empty board on rotatable platform
2. Each player takes their colored pieces
3. Black goes first

## Rules
1. On your turn:
   - Place one piece on the board
   - Roll gravity die
   - Rotate board so indicated direction is "down"
   - All pieces slide down until blocked
2. Pieces stack if they land on each other
3. Once placed, pieces only move via gravity shifts

## Winning Condition
First to get 4 of your pieces in a row (horizontal, vertical, or diagonal) after a gravity shift wins.

## Special Rules
- Stacked pieces move together
- Can strategically place to block opponent alignments
- If board becomes full with no winner, highest stack of your color wins
- Advanced: some spaces have "magnetic" properties (resist gravity)

## Strategic Elements
- Must visualize post-gravity positions
- Can use opponent pieces as anchors
- Timing: when to create your alignment vs. disrupting opponents
