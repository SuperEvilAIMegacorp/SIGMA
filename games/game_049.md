# Fortress Siege

## Game Information
- **Genre**: Asymmetric Strategy
- **Players**: 2
- **Duration**: 30-40 minutes
- **Complexity**: 7/10 (Complex)

## Objective
**Defender:** Survive 10 rounds
**Attacker:** Breach fortress before round 10

## Components
- Fortress board (walls, towers, gates)
- Defender units (20 soldiers, 3 cannons)
- Attacker units (30 soldiers, 2 siege engines, 1 battering ram)
- Action cards (different for each side)
- Resource trackers

## Setup
1. Defender places units inside fortress
2. Attacker places units at siege line (outside range)
3. Both players draw 5 action cards

## Rules
**Turn Structure:**
1. **Draw Phase:** Draw 1 action card
2. **Action Phase:** Play up to 3 action cards
3. **Combat Phase:** Resolve attacks
4. **Refresh Phase:** Ready units

**Defender Actions:**
- Repair walls (restore HP)
- Position archers (ranged attack)
- Boost morale (bonus defense)

**Attacker Actions:**
- Advance units (move closer)
- Assault walls (deal damage)
- Deploy siege weapons

## Winning Condition
**Defender:** Survive 10 rounds with gate intact
**Attacker:** Destroy gate before round 10

## Special Rules
- Walls have HP (100), gate has HP (80)
- Siege engines deal heavy damage but slow
- Cannons counter siege engines
- Morale system: losses affect combat effectiveness
- Weather cards: random events each round
