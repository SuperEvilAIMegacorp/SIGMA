# Impulse Market

## Game Information
- **Genre**: Real-Time Economics
- **Players**: 3-6
- **Duration**: 20-30 minutes
- **Complexity**: 5/10 (Medium)

## Objective
Accumulate the most wealth by trading goods in a real-time, fluctuating market.

## Components
- 120 commodity cards (6 types, 20 each)
- Price board (live market display)
- Trading bell
- Money tokens (starting: 100 per player)
- Event cards (market shocks)
- Trading contracts

## Setup
1. Each player starts with 100 money and 5 random commodities
2. Set initial market prices (randomized)
3. Shuffle event deck
4. Place trading bell in center

## Rules
**Real-Time Trading:**
- Game runs continuously for 20 minutes
- Players can trade anytime by:
  - Ringing bell to propose trade
  - Announcing offer: "Selling 2 wheat for 15 gold"
  - First to accept gets the trade
- Buy from/sell to market at current prices

**Market Fluctuations:**
- Every 2 minutes, draw event card
- Events shift prices:
  - Drought: grain prices up 50%
  - Discovery: ore prices down 30%
  - Embargo: can't trade certain goods for 1 minute
- Supply/demand: prices adjust based on trades

**Contracts:**
- Can make future commitments:
  - "I'll buy your wheat in 5 minutes for X price"
  - Binding agreements (honor system or arbiter)
  - Risk: prices may change

## Winning Condition
Most total wealth (money + commodity values at final prices) when timer expires.

## Special Rules
- **Market Manipulation:**
  - Buying heavily affects prices (up 10%)
  - Selling heavily affects prices (down 10%)
  - Can intentionally manipulate for profit
- **Speculation:**
  - Can announce false information (bluffing)
  - Risk: reputation damage if caught
- **Trading Frenzy:**
  - When bell rings, pause all trades
  - Complete announced trade first
  - Prevents chaos
- **Bankruptcy Protection:**
  - If wealth drops below 20, get 30-coin loan (-40 at game end)

## Commodity Types
- Grain (stable, necessary)
- Ore (volatile, industrial)
- Gems (luxury, high value)
- Textiles (moderate)
- Spices (rare, erratic)
- Livestock (slow-growing value)

## Strategic Elements
- Timing purchases/sales
- Reading event patterns
- Negotiation skills
- Risk tolerance
- Market psychology
- Portfolio diversification

## Variants
- Team mode: pairs share resources
- Insider trading: some players get event previews
- Futures contracts: formal betting on future prices
