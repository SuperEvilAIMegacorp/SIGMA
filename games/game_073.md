# Flavor Fusion

## Game Information
- **Genre**: Creative Combination
- **Players**: 3-6
- **Duration**: 25-30 minutes
- **Complexity**: 2/10 (Simple)

## Objective
Create the most appealing flavor combinations and win taste tests.

## Components
- 100 ingredient cards (fruits, spices, proteins, herbs, etc.)
- 30 dish type cards (dessert, main course, appetizer, etc.)
- Voting tokens
- Presentation board

## Setup
1. Shuffle ingredient deck
2. Each player draws 7 ingredient cards
3. Shuffle dish type deck

## Rules
1. Reveal one dish type card
2. Players have 2 minutes to select 3-5 ingredients from their hand
3. Each player presents their dish:
   - Name it
   - Describe why ingredients work together
   - Explain preparation method
4. All players vote for best dish (can't vote for own)
5. Winner scores 2 points, draws 3 cards
6. Everyone else draws 1 card

## Winning Condition
First to 10 points wins.

## Special Rules
- Wildcard ingredient: can be anything you name
- Critic card: veto one ingredient before voting (rare draw)
- Fusion bonus: combining ingredients from different cuisines = +1 point
- Judges can challenge unrealistic combinations (group vote)

## Presentation Tips
- Creativity matters
- Flavor balance (sweet, salty, sour, bitter, umami)
- Cultural authenticity vs creative liberty
- Texture contrast
