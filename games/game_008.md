# Pattern Grid

## Game Information
- **Genre**: Abstract Strategy
- **Players**: 2
- **Duration**: 10-15 minutes
- **Complexity**: 3/10 (Simple-Medium)

## Objective
Create a pattern of 4 of your pieces in a row (horizontal, vertical, or diagonal).

## Components
- 5x5 grid board
- 12 black pieces
- 12 white pieces

## Setup
1. Empty board placed between players
2. Each player takes their colored pieces
3. Black goes first

## Rules
1. On your turn, place one piece on any empty space
2. Once placed, pieces cannot be moved
3. Players alternate turns

## Winning Condition
First player to get 4 pieces in a row wins.

## Special Rules
- If the board fills with no winner, the game is a draw
- Diagonal lines count in all directions
