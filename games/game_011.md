# Color Zones

## Game Information
- **Genre**: Territory Control
- **Players**: 2-4
- **Duration**: 20-30 minutes
- **Complexity**: 4/10 (Medium)

## Objective
Control the most territory on the board by the end of the game.

## Components
- 8x8 grid board
- 16 pieces per player (4 different colors)
- 20 zone tokens

## Setup
1. Each player starts in a corner of the board
2. Place 3 starting pieces in your corner zone
3. Shuffle zone tokens face-down

## Rules
1. On your turn, either:
   - Place one piece on an empty adjacent space (orthogonally)
   - Move one of your pieces to an adjacent empty space
2. Draw one zone token if available
3. Zone tokens let you claim special abilities or extra spaces
4. Adjacent pieces of the same color form controlled zones

## Winning Condition
After all 64 spaces are filled, player controlling the most spaces wins.

## Special Rules
- You cannot move into spaces occupied by opponents
- Zone tokens can be used anytime on your turn
- Each zone token has unique effects (draw 2 when you get one, choose 1)
