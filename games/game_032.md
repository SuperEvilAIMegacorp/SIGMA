# Animal Safari

## Game Information
- **Genre**: Memory & Set Collection
- **Players**: 2-4
- **Duration**: 15-20 minutes
- **Complexity**: 2/10 (Simple)

## Objective
Collect the most complete sets of animal families.

## Components
- 48 animal cards (6 families, 8 cards each with different attributes)
- Watering hole board (4x4 grid)

## Setup
1. Shuffle animal cards face-down in 4x4 grid (watering hole)
2. Remaining cards form draw pile
3. Determine starting player

## Rules
1. On your turn, flip 2 cards face-up
2. If they match (same animal family):
   - Take both cards
   - Take another turn
3. If they don't match:
   - Flip back face-down
   - Replace from draw pile if needed
4. Remember locations for future turns

## Winning Condition
When all cards collected, player with most complete families wins.

## Special Rules
- Complete family = all 8 cards (scored as 10 points)
- Incomplete families score 1 point per card
- Golden animal card = wild (completes any family)
