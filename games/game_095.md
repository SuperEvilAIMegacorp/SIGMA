# Temporal Tactics

## Game Information
- **Genre**: Turn-Based Strategy
- **Players**: 2
- **Duration**: 40-50 minutes
- **Complexity**: 9/10 (Very Complex)

## Objective
Defeat opponent by controlling the timeline and achieving victory in multiple time periods simultaneously.

## Components
- 3 era boards (Past, Present, Future)
- 30 unit tokens per player (different types)
- Timeline tracker
- Action cards (60)
- Paradox tokens
- Causality chains

## Setup
1. Arrange 3 era boards vertically
2. Each player deploys starting units across eras
3. Shuffle action deck
4. Draw 5 action cards each
5. Paradox tracker at 0

## Rules
**Complex Turn Structure:**
1. **Timeline Phase:**
   - Choose which era to act in (Past, Present, or Future)
   - Actions in Past can affect Present and Future
2. **Action Phase:**
   - Play action cards (move, attack, abilities)
   - Each era has different tactical rules
   - Units have era-specific stats
3. **Causality Phase:**
   - Changes in Past propagate forward
   - Must maintain causality (effects follow causes)
   - Violations create paradoxes
4. **Paradox Resolution:**
   - Too many paradoxes = lose
   - Can "spend" paradoxes for powerful effects

## Era Rules
**Past:**
- Units are weak but actions echo forward
- Controlling Past strongholds grants Present reinforcements

**Present:**
- Standard tactical combat
- Most straightforward mechanics
- Resource generation

**Future:**
- Advanced units and technology
- Can send intelligence back to Past (limited)
- Unstable (affected by all previous actions)

## Causality Chains
- Destroying Past unit removes its Future descendants
- Building Past structure enables Future upgrades
- Complex cascades possible

## Winning Condition
Control 2 of 3 eras simultaneously OR reduce opponent to 0 units in all eras.

## Special Rules
- **Temporal Paradoxes:**
  - Effect without cause: +1 paradox
  - Circular causality: +2 paradoxes
  - Can intentionally create paradoxes for advantage (risky)
- **Time Travel:**
  - Some units can move between eras
  - Costs resources, creates vulnerability
- **Prophecy Mechanic:**
  - See opponent's Future moves
  - Can prepare counter-strategies in Present
- **Grandfather Paradox:**
  - Special win condition: eliminate Past unit that caused opponent's Present army

## Strategic Depth
- Multi-era planning
- Causality management
- Paradox risk/reward
- Timeline forking strategies
- Defensive positioning across time
- Temporal pincer movements

## Complexity Warning
This game requires:
- Tracking multiple board states
- Understanding causality chains
- Planning 3-5 turns ahead across eras
- Managing paradox accumulation
- Visualizing timeline alterations
