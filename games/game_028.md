# Detective's Notes

## Game Information
- **Genre**: Deduction Game
- **Players**: 3-5
- **Duration**: 40-50 minutes
- **Complexity**: 7/10 (Complex)

## Objective
Solve the mystery by deducing WHO did it, WHERE, with WHAT weapon, and WHY.

## Components
- 6 suspect cards
- 8 location cards
- 6 weapon cards
- 5 motive cards
- Case file envelope (contains 1 of each category)
- Player notebook sheets

## Setup
1. Secretly place 1 card of each category in case file
2. Shuffle remaining cards together
3. Deal cards evenly to all players
4. Players examine their cards (these are eliminated from solution)

## Rules
1. On your turn, move to a location and make a suggestion:
   - "I suspect [WHO] did it at [WHERE] with [WEAPON] because [MOTIVE]"
2. Next player clockwise must reveal one matching card if possible
3. If they can't, next player tries (continue around table)
4. Take notes on what you learn
5. Once per game, you can make an accusation (all 4 categories)

## Winning Condition
Make correct accusation. Incorrect accusation = eliminated (but still reveal cards).

## Special Rules
- Must move to different location each turn
- Can strategically suggest cards you hold
- Game ends when someone makes correct accusation or all eliminated
