# Merchant Caravan

## Game Information
- **Genre**: Pick-up and Deliver
- **Players**: 2-4
- **Duration**: 45-60 minutes
- **Complexity**: 6/10 (Medium-Complex)

## Objective
Earn the most profit by transporting goods across the trade network.

## Components
- Map board (20 cities connected by roads)
- Goods tokens (6 types)
- Caravan meeples (3 per player)
- Demand cards (what each city wants)
- Event cards (robberies, weather, etc.)
- Starting capital: 200 gold per player

## Setup
1. Place cities on map
2. Shuffle and place demand cards at each city
3. Each player places caravans at starting city
4. Draw 3 goods randomly for starting inventory

## Rules
1. **Movement Phase:** Move each caravan up to 3 roads
2. **Trading Phase:** At cities:
   - Buy goods (price varies by city)
   - Sell goods (bonus if it matches demand card)
3. **Event Phase:** Draw event card (affects all players)
4. **Upgrade Phase:** Spend gold on:
   - Larger caravans (carry more)
   - Faster movement
   - Guards (protection from robberies)

## Winning Condition
Most gold after 15 rounds wins.

## Special Rules
- Caravan capacity: 5 goods (upgradeable to 10)
- Demand cards refresh each round
- Cities have limited supply (race to buy)
- Risk/reward: dangerous roads have better prices
- Can form temporary trading alliances
