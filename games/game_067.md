# Midnight Masquerade

## Game Information
- **Genre**: Hidden Identity
- **Players**: 6-10
- **Duration**: 40-60 minutes
- **Complexity**: 7/10 (Complex)

## Objective
Complete your secret role's objectives while identifying others' roles.

## Components
- 10 unique role cards (each with different win condition)
- Interaction cards (40)
- Accusation tokens (3 per player)
- Identity reveal tokens
- Information tracker sheets

## Setup
1. Each player draws secret role card
2. Shuffle interaction deck
3. Put on masks (optional thematic element)
4. Each player gets 3 accusation tokens

## Rules
**Turn Structure:**
1. **Mingling Phase (3 minutes):**
   - Move around, interact with other players
   - Can make claims about your role (truth or lies)
   - Gather information
2. **Action Phase:**
   - Play interaction cards
   - Cards let you: trade items, make alliances, gather clues, sabotage
3. **Accusation Phase:**
   - Can spend token to accuse someone's role
   - If correct: that player's role revealed to you only (+3 points)
   - If wrong: you must reveal your role to them (-2 points)
4. Play until someone completes win condition or 8 rounds pass

## Sample Roles
- **The Noble:** Survive to the end without being accused correctly
- **The Thief:** Collect 5 specific items without being caught
- **The Detective:** Correctly identify 3 other players' roles
- **The Spy:** Form alliance with the Assassin (neither knowing initially)
- **The Assassin:** Eliminate the Noble (via accusation mechanics)

## Winning Condition
First to complete role objective wins. Multiple winners possible if objectives align.

## Special Rules
- Role synergies and conflicts
- Some roles can change mid-game
- Hidden objectives revealed upon completion
- Bluffing encouraged
