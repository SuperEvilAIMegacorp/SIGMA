# Forbidden Library

## Game Information
- **Genre**: Cooperative Adventure
- **Players**: 1-4
- **Duration**: 60-90 minutes
- **Complexity**: 7/10 (Complex)

## Objective
Explore the magical library, collect knowledge tomes, and escape before it collapses.

## Components
- 40 room tiles (modular dungeon)
- 30 knowledge tome cards
- Character sheets (4 unique characters)
- Event deck (hazards, discoveries, encounters)
- Collapse tracker (starts at 30)
- Spell cards
- Enemy tokens

## Setup
1. Each player chooses character with unique ability
2. Place entrance tile, shuffle remaining rooms
3. Shuffle tome deck
4. Each player draws 2 spell cards
5. Collapse tracker at 30

## Rules
**Turn Structure:**
1. **Movement:** Move up to 3 spaces
2. **Action:** Take one action:
   - Search room (draw tome if available)
   - Cast spell (use spell card)
   - Assist ally (help adjacent player)
   - Rest (recover health)
3. **Event:** Draw event card
4. **Collapse:** Tracker decreases by 1 (or more with certain events)

**Room Exploration:**
- Flip new room tile when entering unexplored area
- Some rooms have enemies (combat required)
- Some rooms have puzzles (must solve to proceed)
- Some rooms contain rare tomes

**Combat:**
- Roll character die + spell modifiers
- Enemy rolls its die
- Higher result wins, difference = damage dealt

## Winning Condition
Collect 10 knowledge tomes and reach exit before collapse reaches 0.

## Special Rules
- Characters have synergistic abilities
- Some tomes unlock powerful spells
- Can sacrifice tomes to prevent collapse (emergency measure)
- Secret knowledge: some tomes chain together for massive bonus
- Forbidden tomes: powerful but accelerate collapse

## Characters
- Scholar: bonus on puzzles
- Mage: extra spell slots
- Guardian: combat bonuses
- Scout: extra movement
